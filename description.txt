Survive It
A game by Henry Bailey 9yrs old.


You are a one of Gru's minions, you must find the ancient temple somewhere in the haunted woods where dangerous monsters lurke.
Once in the temple, find the cage and free Gru's trapped son (Felonius). 


Characters
(See characters.pdf) 
You are a minion. 
Gru gives you your mission and guidance
Felonius is Gru's son who needs to be rescued
The evil bird Roro guards the temples cage and is a final boss


Graphics
The game is 3D first person, realistic look


Mechanics
YOu have a health bar which monsters can deplete
Pick up med kits top up health
Collect minion style weapons to kill the monsters
Find a key to unlock the temple, then 4 more keys to unlock hidden chambers within the temple and finally one more key to unlock the cage.


Platform
The game should run in a browser and use mouse and keyboard controls
